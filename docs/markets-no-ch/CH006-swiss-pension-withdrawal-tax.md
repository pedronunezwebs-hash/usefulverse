# CH006 · Swiss pension capital withdrawal tax comparison

## Purpose and jurisdiction

This is a Switzerland-specific comparison page. It does not infer Swiss tax rules from the visitor's interface language or location. The visitor enters the combined pension capital benefits due in each tax year and the corresponding result from the current official ESTV calculator. This site calculates only the effective share, after-tax amount and differences between entered scenarios; it does not reproduce cantonal tax tables or calculate a tax assessment.

## Aggregation and limits

ESTV's calculation notes state that pension capital benefits due in the same tax year are added together and taxed in one separate annual assessment. Scenarios with a different year, municipality or household profile remain visible but are explicitly marked as not directly comparable. The tool does not determine whether, when or how a pension provider permits staged withdrawals, and it is not advice on choosing capital versus an annuity.

## Current official sources

- [ESTV · tax calculator and comparisons](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuerrechner-steuern-berechnen) — links to the live official calculator.
- [ESTV · calculation notes](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/4wCmxZ0qNcsD/Erlaeuterungen-Steuerberechnungen_de-fr-it-en.pdf) — defines eligible pension capital benefits and same-year aggregation.
- [ch.ch · third pillar](https://www.ch.ch/en/taxes-and-finances/old-age-pension/3rd-pillar/) — explains one-off withdrawal taxation and directs users to the cantonal authority/provider for individual conditions.

The calculator is the updating source for the user's municipality and year. No extracted tax table, paid data feed or visitor-time network call is used. The comparison and CSV export run locally in the browser.

## Global interface behavior

The 37 language versions translate the interface only. They continue to name Switzerland, CHF, ESTV and the Swiss municipal/household scope. A translation never changes the legal jurisdiction or assumes that the visitor is Swiss tax-resident.
