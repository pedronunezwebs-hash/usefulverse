The site bundles this North American English dictionary for local lookups. UsefulVerse maps its ARPAbet phones to a broad General American IPA rendering; that conversion is not part of CMUdict. The dictionary maintainers do not guarantee accuracy or suitability. This snapshot is not a comprehensive list of English words or accents.
