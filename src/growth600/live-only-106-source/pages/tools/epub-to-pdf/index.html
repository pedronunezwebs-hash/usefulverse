EPUB to PDF Converter

Open a DRM-free EPUB on this device, then use your browser’s print dialog to save a text-first PDF. Chapter order follows the book. Original layout, images and embedded fonts are not preserved.

Maximum file size: 25 MB. Expanded book content is limited to protect browser memory.

Text-first conversion: chapter order and readable text are kept. Original page design, pictures, tables, footnotes, embedded fonts and interactive content may not be preserved. DRM-protected or malformed EPUB files are not supported. Large books may use significant memory; a computer is recommended.

Print preview

How do I save an EPUB as a PDF?

Choose a DRM-free EPUB up to 25 MB. After the book is read locally, select Prepare PDF. In the print dialog choose Save as PDF, then choose a destination on your device.

Frequently asked questions

How do I save an EPUB as a PDF?

Choose a DRM-free EPUB up to 25 MB. After the book is read locally, select Prepare PDF. In the print dialog choose Save as PDF, then choose a destination on your device.

Is my EPUB uploaded?

No. The file is read in this browser and is not sent to UsefulVerse. Your browser opens a local print document that you can save as PDF.

Will the PDF look exactly like the EPUB?

No. This creates a clean, text-first printable copy in chapter order. Original styling, images, tables, footnotes and embedded fonts may be omitted.

Can it convert a DRM-protected book?

No. DRM removal is not supported. Use only books you are allowed to convert. The browser print dialog opens; choose Save as PDF and pick where to save it.
