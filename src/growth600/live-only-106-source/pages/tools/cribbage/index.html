Can I play a full Cribbage game here?

Start a local match, discard two of your six cards to the crib, then play legal cards toward 31. Score fifteens, pairs and runs; say go when you cannot play. Count the non-dealer hand, dealer hand and crib. The default target is 121 points and can be lowered for a shorter match.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play a full Cribbage game here?

Start a local match, discard two of your six cards to the crib, then play legal cards toward 31. Score fifteens, pairs and runs; say go when you cannot play. Count the non-dealer hand, dealer hand and crib. The default target is 121 points and can be lowered for a shorter match.

Does this cribbage scorer upload my cards?

No. The match and scoring run in this browser. There is no online opponent, account, upload or saved match.

How many points is a 15 in cribbage?

Each distinct combination of cards that totals 15 scores two points. Several different combinations can score in the same hand.

How is a crib flush counted?

All four crib cards and the starter must share a suit for five points. Unlike a regular hand, four matching crib cards without the starter do not score a flush.

Can I play against another person?

No. The match is local against a simple browser opponent. There is no online multiplayer or saved match.
