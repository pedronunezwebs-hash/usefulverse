How do you play the snake game?

Start the round, steer toward the food using arrow keys, WASD or the touch buttons, and avoid the walls and your body. Pause any time. Eating food grows the snake and adds one point.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play the snake game?

Start the round, steer toward the food using arrow keys, WASD or the touch buttons, and avoid the walls and your body. Pause any time. Eating food grows the snake and adds one point.

Does the snake game save my score or use an account?

No account or download is needed. Your current score and session best stay in memory in this tab only; they are not uploaded or saved after you leave.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Use the on-screen direction buttons. A keyboard with arrow keys or WASD also works on a computer.

Can I pause the game?

Yes. Select Pause at any time; the snake stops moving until you resume.

Will I get the same game every time?

The board layout stays familiar, but the food position is randomized for each new round. The current and best session scores are not saved after you leave.
