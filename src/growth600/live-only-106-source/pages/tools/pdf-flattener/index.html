PDF Form Flattener

Make a separate PDF copy with supported AcroForm fields flattened into their visible page appearance. Text on the page remains selectable and searchable.

Flattening removes form interactivity in the new copy. Keep your original. Existing digital signatures may be invalidated or removed; this is not a signature validation tool.

Local limits: 25 MiB input, 300 pages, 2,500 fields and 50 MiB output. Complex, encrypted or XFA forms may not be supported. A computer is recommended for large files.

Only supported AcroForm fields are flattened. Other comments, highlights, links, stamps, metadata and accessibility structure are not flattened or guaranteed to remain unchanged.

What does flattening a PDF form do?

It draws supported interactive form-field appearances into the page content and removes those interactive fields from the new copy. This does not flatten general comments, links, stamps or other markup annotations.

Frequently asked questions

What does flattening a PDF form do?

It draws supported interactive form-field appearances into the page content and removes those interactive fields from the new copy. This does not flatten general comments, links, stamps or other markup annotations.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Processing runs in this browser. Your selected PDF is not sent to a server or automatically saved.

Can I still edit the fields in the flattened copy?

No. Supported fields become part of the page appearance and are no longer interactive. Keep the original if you may need to edit it later.

Does this flatten comments, highlights or digital signatures?

No. This tool targets supported AcroForm fields only. Other markup annotations are not flattened, and digital signatures may be invalidated or removed by editing. Do not use this for certified or legally signed documents.

Will the page text remain searchable?

The page content is not converted to an image, so existing page text remains searchable. Form-field appearances are flattened into page content.
