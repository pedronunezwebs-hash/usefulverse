      Match result

      Standard 24-card North American scoring: a made contract earns 1 point, a march earns 2 and a euchred team gives opponents 2. This version does not offer loner bids or online multiplayer. Bot decisions are deliberately simple.

      Show rules

      Each hand uses 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King and Ace in all four suits. The right bower is the Jack of trump; the left bower is the Jack of the same color and also counts as trump. Players must follow the effective suit led when able. A team that makes trump takes at least three tricks. First to the selected target wins.

      How do you play Euchre here?

      Pass or order up the turned-up suit in round one. If everyone passes, name a different trump suit in round two. The dealer takes the upcard and discards one card. Play five tricks while following the effective suit; the left bower counts as trump. The first team to 10 points wins.

      Frequently asked questions

      How do you play Euchre here?

      Pass or order up the turned-up suit in round one. If everyone passes, name a different trump suit in round two. The dealer takes the upcard and discards one card. Play five tricks while following the effective suit; the left bower counts as trump. The first team to 10 points wins.

      Does this Euchre game connect to other players?

      No. The opponents are simple bots running in this browser. Cards and scores are not uploaded or saved by UsefulVerse.

      What is the left bower in Euchre?

      It is the Jack of the same color as trump. It moves into the trump suit for both ranking and following suit, and ranks just below the right bower.

      How does Euchre scoring work in this game?

      The makers earn one point for taking three or four tricks, two for all five, and the defenders earn two if the makers take fewer than three. The selected target is 5, 7 or 10 points.

      Can I play Euchre with people online here?

      No. This version is a local single-player game against three simple bots; it has no online matchmaking or multiplayer connection.
