Car Lease Calculator

Estimate monthly lease payments from the capitalized cost, residual value, lease length, money factor, any cap-cost reduction, and a tax rate you enter.

Use one currency for every money amount. Enter the money factor as a decimal, such as 0.002.

Adjusted cost = capitalized cost − reduction. Monthly depreciation = (adjusted cost − residual) ÷ months. Monthly rent charge = (adjusted cost + residual) × money factor. Tax is applied only to the estimated pre-tax monthly payment.

Illustrative arithmetic only. A lease quote can include fees, taxes on different bases, incentives, mileage limits, due-at-signing amounts and local rules not modeled here. Verify every number with the lessor.

Your entries stay in this browser tab. They are not uploaded or automatically saved.

Lease estimate

How is a car lease payment estimated?

The estimate separates monthly depreciation from the rent charge: depreciation is adjusted capitalized cost minus residual value, divided by the term; the rent charge is their sum multiplied by the money factor. An optional entered tax rate is applied to the pre-tax payment.

Frequently asked questions

How is a car lease payment estimated?

The estimate separates monthly depreciation from the rent charge: depreciation is adjusted capitalized cost minus residual value, divided by the term; the rent charge is their sum multiplied by the money factor. An optional entered tax rate is applied to the pre-tax payment.

Are my lease details uploaded?

No. The estimate runs in this browser tab. Inputs are not uploaded or automatically saved.

What is the money factor?

It is a lease pricing input used by this estimate in the rent-charge formula. Enter the factor from a quote; this tool does not convert an APR or verify a lender’s method.

Does the monthly estimate include every lease charge?

No. The optional tax is applied to this estimate’s pre-tax payment only. Registration, acquisition and disposition fees, insurance, mileage charges, local tax treatment, incentives and amounts due at signing may change an actual quote.

Is this a loan payment calculator?

No. A lease estimate includes a residual value and a rent charge. It does not calculate ownership, a loan amortization schedule, vehicle value, or whether a lease is affordable.
