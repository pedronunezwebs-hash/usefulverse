How are Texas Hold’em odds calculated?

The calculator removes your cards and the visible board from a standard 52-card deck, then compares the best five-card poker hand each player can make. A complete board with one opponent checks every possible two-card hand. Other cases use a seeded Monte Carlo sample, so the same inputs and seed give the same estimate. Equity includes your share of split pots.

Frequently asked questions

Are my poker cards uploaded?

No. Card validation and all calculations run locally in this browser. No account, betting service, network lookup or saved hand history is used.

What does poker equity mean?

Equity is the estimated share of the pot: a win counts as one full share, and a tie counts as an equal fraction for each tied player. It is not the same as win percentage.

When are poker odds exact?

With all five community cards visible and one opponent, the tool checks every two-card opponent hand not already visible. Other situations use a seeded Monte Carlo estimate and can vary from the real outcome.

Can I use this for betting advice?

No. It only compares card combinations under standard Texas Hold’em rules. It does not model betting, opponent behavior, rake, tournament strategy or future decisions.
