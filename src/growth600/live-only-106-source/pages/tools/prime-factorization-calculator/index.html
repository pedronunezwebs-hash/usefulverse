How do you find the prime factorization of a number?

Divide by the smallest prime that divides the number, then repeat with the quotient until the remaining value is prime. For example, 360 = 2³ × 3² × 5.

Frequently asked questions

How do you find the prime factorization of a number?

Divide by the smallest prime that divides the number, then repeat with the quotient until the remaining value is prime. For example, 360 = 2³ × 3² × 5.

Are my numbers uploaded?

No. The calculation runs in this browser and this tool does not upload or save your input.

What is the prime factorization of 360?

360 = 2³ × 3² × 5. Its repeated-prime form is 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5.

How many divisors does a number have?

If its prime powers are pᵃ × qᵇ, the divisor count is (a + 1)(b + 1). This calculator shows the count for the supported positive integer.

Can it factor any size of number?

This browser tool accepts whole numbers from 1 through 1,000,000,000,000. Larger values are outside its stated range.
