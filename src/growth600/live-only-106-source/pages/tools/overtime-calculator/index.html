Overtime Calculator

Enter your weekly hours, hourly pay, overtime threshold, and multiplier to estimate regular pay and overtime pay separately.

Enter the threshold, multiplier, and pay rules that apply to your situation. The calculator does not assume a currency or determine legal eligibility.

No country or employer rules are assumed. This is arithmetic based only on your entries, not a payroll statement, legal advice, tax estimate, or guarantee of wages.

Inputs stay in this browser tab and are not stored or uploaded.

Pay estimate

How do I calculate overtime pay?

This calculator applies the threshold and overtime multiplier you enter: regular hours are paid at the entered hourly rate, and hours above the threshold use the entered multiplier.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate overtime pay?

This calculator applies the threshold and overtime multiplier you enter: regular hours are paid at the entered hourly rate, and hours above the threshold use the entered multiplier.

Is my pay information uploaded?

No. The estimate runs in this browser. Inputs are not saved or uploaded.

Does this calculator apply a specific country’s overtime law?

No. It uses only the threshold and multiplier you enter. Legal rules can depend on location, job classification, agreement, and other details.

How is overtime pay estimated?

Hours at or below your entered threshold use the hourly rate. Hours above it use the hourly rate multiplied by your selected overtime multiplier.

Does the estimate include taxes or deductions?

No. It shows gross arithmetic from the entered rate and hours, before taxes, deductions, bonuses, or other payroll items.
