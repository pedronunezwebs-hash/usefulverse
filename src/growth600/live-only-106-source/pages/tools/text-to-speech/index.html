Text to Speech

Speech playback

How do I convert text to speech online?

Enter or paste text, choose an available voice and press Play. You can pause, continue or stop at any time. Playback uses the speech service selected in your browser or device.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert text to speech online?

Enter or paste text, choose an available voice and press Play. You can pause, continue or stop at any time. Playback uses the speech service selected in your browser or device.

Is my text uploaded or saved?

This site does not upload or save your text. The text is passed to the speech service selected in your browser or device. A voice marked local may process on your device; an unknown or remote voice may send text to its provider. Read that provider’s privacy terms before choosing that voice.

Does this create an MP3 file?

No. Browser speech synthesis plays audio but does not provide a consistent audio-file export. Use a separate recording tool only if you have permission to record the audio.

Why do I see no voices?

Voices come from your browser and operating system. They may load after a short delay, or none may be installed for this language. Check device speech settings or try another browser.

Does a local voice guarantee that audio stays on my device?

No. This site does not upload your text, but the browser’s local label cannot guarantee how the operating system or speech service processes audio. An unknown or remote voice may send text to its provider.
