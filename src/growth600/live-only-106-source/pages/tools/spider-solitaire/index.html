Spider Solitaire

Build descending sequences in the same card suit from King to Ace, reveal face-down cards and complete all eight sequences. Choose one, two or four card suits.

Leave blank for a fresh random game. Reuse the same seed to reproduce the same shuffled deal; it stays only in this page.

Completed sequences: 0/8

Moves: 0

Only face-up descending groups in the same card suit can move together. A single face-up card can move by itself. Face-down cards turn over when exposed.

Completed runs are removed automatically. Empty columns accept any face-up card or valid run.

How do you play Spider Solitaire?

Move face-up descending sequences of the same card suit onto the next higher rank or an empty column. Reveal hidden cards, draw ten cards from the stock pile when every column is occupied, and complete eight same-suit King-to-Ace sequences to win.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Spider Solitaire?

Move face-up descending sequences of the same card suit onto the next higher rank or an empty column. Reveal hidden cards, draw ten cards from the stock pile when every column is occupied, and complete eight same-suit King-to-Ace sequences to win.

Does Spider Solitaire save my game?

No. The game runs in this tab and does not save a game, use an account, upload moves or send them to a server. Starting a new deal replaces the current game.

What is the difference between one, two and four card suits?

One card suit is the most relaxed mode, two card suits add another suit, and four card suits require matching the suit across a mixed-suit tableau.

When can I draw cards from the stock pile?

Draw ten cards, one to each column, while stock remains. Every tableau column must contain at least one card before drawing from the stock pile.

How does undo work?

Undo restores the exact previous game state for a move, stock deal or completed-run removal. Up to 100 recent actions are kept only in this tab.
