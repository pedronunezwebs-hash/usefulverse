How do I calculate fuel economy from a refill?

Enter the distance driven since the previous full tank, the fuel added at this refill and the total price paid. For a useful result, start and end each interval with a full tank. The calculator converts the same entry to L/100 km, km/L, US MPG and imperial MPG.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate fuel economy from a refill?

Enter the distance driven since the previous full tank, the fuel added at this refill and the total price paid. For a useful result, start and end each interval with a full tank. The calculator converts the same entry to L/100 km, km/L, US MPG and imperial MPG.

Does the fuel log leave this page?

No. Entries stay in this browser tab and are cleared when you clear them or close the page. Export a CSV yourself if you want a separate copy. No account, upload or fuel-price service is used.

What does L/100 km mean?

It is the litres of fuel used to travel 100 kilometres. A lower number means less fuel for the same distance.

How do I convert fuel economy to US MPG?

Choose the distance and fuel units for your entry. The calculator converts the same measured interval to US MPG and imperial MPG; those gallon sizes are different.

Why should I use full-to-full refills?

Starting and ending with a full tank makes the fuel added a closer measure of what was used over that distance. One partial refill can make an interval less accurate.
