Frequently asked questions

How do I make an email signature?

Add your name and optional contact details, choose an accent color, then generate the signature. Copy the formatted block into your email settings or copy its HTML if your email app supports it.

Are my details uploaded?

No. Your details and optional logo are handled in this browser tab and are not uploaded or automatically saved.

Will this signature look identical in every email app?

No. Email apps support different HTML and image features. This generator uses a compact table and inline styles, but you should test the pasted signature in your own email app.

Can I use a logo in my signature?

Yes. Choose a PNG, JPEG, or GIF up to 512 KiB. It is embedded as image data; some email apps block or remove embedded images, so a text-only signature may be more reliable.

Does the generator send or save my contact details?

No. It builds the signature in this browser tab. You choose when to copy or download it; this page does not upload or automatically save your details.
