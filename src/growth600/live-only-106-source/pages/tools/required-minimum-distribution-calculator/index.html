How do I estimate a 2026 required minimum distribution?

Divide the eligible account balance from December 31, 2025 by the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table factor for your age in 2026. This calculator applies Table III only and does not decide whether an RMD is due.

Frequently asked questions

How do I estimate a 2026 required minimum distribution?

Divide the eligible account balance from December 31, 2025 by the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table factor for your age in 2026. This calculator applies Table III only and does not decide whether an RMD is due.

Are my retirement figures uploaded?

No. The calculation runs in this browser. The balance and age you enter are not uploaded or saved by this tool.

What balance and age does a 2026 RMD estimate use?

For this illustration, enter the account balance from December 31, 2025 and your age on your birthday in 2026. IRS rules and account type determine which table and timing apply.

Can I use this for an inherited IRA or a much younger spouse?

Not reliably. Inherited accounts may use different rules and tables. A sole spouse beneficiary more than 10 years younger generally uses the Joint Life and Last Survivor Table, not Table III.

Does this tell me when or whether I must take an RMD?

No. It only divides a balance by a Table III factor. It does not determine your required beginning date, eligibility, deadlines, aggregation rules, plan exceptions, or tax consequences.
