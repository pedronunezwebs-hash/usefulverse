How does this rhyme finder work?

It groups words by matching their final written letters. It does not compare pronunciation, stress, or syllables, so a shared spelling ending is not a guarantee that words rhyme when spoken.

Frequently asked questions

How does this rhyme finder work?

It groups words by matching their final written letters. It does not compare pronunciation, stress, or syllables, so a shared spelling ending is not a guarantee that words rhyme when spoken.

Are my words uploaded?

No. Text is analyzed in this browser and is not uploaded or saved.

Does this check how words sound?

No. It compares written endings only and does not use a pronunciation dictionary or phonetic transcription.

Can I use words from any language?

Yes. Unicode letters are supported, but results are based on spelling and may not match how words sound in that language.

Can I paste a whole poem?

Yes. The local input is limited to 50,000 characters and 5,000 words.
