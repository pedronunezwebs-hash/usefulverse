Gomoku

Place black and white stones on a 15×15 board. Make five or more in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win. Play a friend on this device or challenge a basic local opponent.

Rules Freestyle Gomoku: five or more continuous stones win. The local opponent uses a small heuristic, not a tournament-strength search. Game state stays in this tab and is cleared on reload.

How do you win at Gomoku?

Take turns placing one stone on an empty intersection. The first player to make five or more continuous stones in any direction wins. This version uses freestyle rules: overlines also win.

Frequently asked questions

How do you win at Gomoku?

Take turns placing one stone on an empty intersection. The first player to make five or more continuous stones in any direction wins. This version uses freestyle rules: overlines also win.

Can I play Gomoku without downloading anything?

Yes. The board and local opponent run in this browser. The game is not uploaded or automatically saved.

Does a line need to be exactly five stones?

No. This game uses freestyle rules: five or more consecutive stones in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal direction wins.

Can I play Gomoku against the computer?

Yes. The local computer checks immediate wins and blocks, then scores nearby open lines. It is a simple opponent, not an advanced or tournament-strength AI.

Does Gomoku work with two players?

Yes. Choose the two-player mode to take turns on the same device. There is no online multiplayer or automatic game saving.
