Crossword Online

Solve a fresh, locally generated crossword from a small set of original clues. Select a clue or square, type with your keyboard, and check or reveal answers when you need help.

Answers ignore spaces and letter case. The clue set is finite and original; a new board rearranges its entries but does not promise an unlimited daily puzzle feed.

Keyboard: type a letter, use arrows to move, and press Backspace to clear a square.

      How do I play this crossword?

      Choose an across or down clue, then type letters into the selected squares. Use the arrow keys to move within the answer. Check a word or the whole puzzle, reveal one letter or the full answer, or start a new shuffled board.

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I play this crossword?

      Choose an across or down clue, then type letters into the selected squares. Use the arrow keys to move within the answer. Check a word or the whole puzzle, reveal one letter or the full answer, or start a new shuffled board.

      Are my answers uploaded or saved?

      No. The puzzle, answers and score stay in this browser tab. There is no account, upload, server processing or saved play history.

      Does this crossword include an unlimited daily puzzle feed?

      No. The page builds shuffled boards from a small, finite set of original clue-and-answer pairs. It does not fetch or claim an unlimited daily puzzle library.

      Can I use this crossword without uploading anything?

      Yes. The board and your answers run in this browser tab. The tool does not upload your play or require an account.

      Is this a crossword maker?

      No. This page is for playing clues. Use the separate Crossword Maker when you want to arrange your own answer-and-clue pairs.
