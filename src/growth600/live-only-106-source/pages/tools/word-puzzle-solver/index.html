Matching words

    How does this word finder work?

    Choose anagram mode to find words that can be made from your letters, or pattern mode to match fixed letters and ? or . placeholders. Results come only from the selected compact built-in list and/or the word list you paste.

    Frequently asked questions

    How does this word finder work?

    Choose anagram mode to find words that can be made from your letters, or pattern mode to match fixed letters and ? or . placeholders. Results come only from the selected compact built-in list and/or the word list you paste.

    Are my letters or word list uploaded?

    No. Searches run locally in this page. Your letters and pasted word list are not uploaded, saved to an account or sent to a server.

    Can I use blank tiles in an anagram?

    Yes. Enter ? for each blank tile. Each ? can stand for one letter. “Use every letter tile” requires a match to use all entered tiles.

    Can this solve a crossword clue by meaning?

    No. It only matches letter patterns and filters words in the selected list; it does not understand clue meanings or provide semantic answers.

    Does the built-in list contain every word?

    No. It is a small multilingual starter vocabulary, not a complete dictionary or official game-word list. Paste your own word list for broader coverage.
