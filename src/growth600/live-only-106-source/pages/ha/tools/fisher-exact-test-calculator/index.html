Yaushe zan yi amfani da ainihin gwajin Fisher?

shine ainihin gwajin haɗin gwiwa don tebur na 2 × 2, galibi ana amfani dashi lokacin da ƙididdige ƙididdiga kaɗan. Sakamakon ya dogara da ƙirar binciken kuma baya tabbatar da dalilin.

An loda kirga na?

A'a. Lissafin yana gudana a cikin wannan mai binciken. Wannan kayan aikin ba a loda ko adana ƙididdiga.

Menene hasashe mara tushe?

Gwajin yana kimanta ko rabe-raben jeri da ginshiƙi masu zaman kansu ne, sharadi akan abubuwan da aka lura. Bai bayyana dalilin da yasa ƙungiya zata iya kasancewa ba.

Yaya ake lissafin p-darajar mai gefe biyu?

Wannan kalkuleta yana ƙara yuwuwar duk yuwuwar teburi tare da gefe guda waɗanda yuwuwar yanayin yanayin bai fi na tebur da aka gani ba. Sauran ƙa'idodin software na iya bambanta.

Shin ƙaramin p-darajar yana tabbatar da tasiri?

A'a. p-darajar ba girman tasiri bane ko hujjar sanadi. Yi nazarin rabon rashin daidaituwa, ƙirar nazari, ingancin bayanai, rashin tabbas da kowane shirin gwaji da yawa.
