Ƙididdigar Yankin Ƙimar Zuciya

Ƙididdiga wuraren motsa jiki guda biyar na motsa jiki daga matsakaicin tushen shekaru ko iyakar da kuka riga kuka sani. Zaɓi kaso-mafi girma ko ajiyar Karvonen, sannan daidaita kowace iyakar yanki.

Ƙimar iyaka

Saita tsantsa guda shida na haɓaka cutoffs daga 0 zuwa 100%. Suna ƙirƙirar yankuna biyar masu kusa; fara da gazawar ko keɓance su.

Ƙididdigar gama gari ita ce matsakaicin ƙimar zuciya ≈ 220 - shekaru, amma ba ma'auni bane kuma yana iya bambanta sosai ga mutum. Tsohuwar 50-100% cutoffs ne kawai ɓangaren gyarawa, ba shawarwarin ko kewayon AHA manufa; AHA yana bayyana maƙasudin gabaɗaya na kusan 50-85% na ƙididdiga mafi girma don matsakaici-zuwa aiki mai ƙarfi.

Magunguna da yanayin kiwon lafiya na iya canza martanin yawan bugun zuciya. Idan kuna da yanayin zuciya, ɗauki magani wanda ke shafar bugun zuciya, kuna da ciki, ba su da shekaru 18, ko kuna buƙatar abin motsa jiki na sirri, tambayi ƙwararren ƙwararren lafiya. Dakatar da aiki kuma nemi taimako da ya dace game da alamun alamun. Gwajin magana na CDC wata hanya ce don yin hukunci game da ƙoƙarin dangi. Saukewa:

Duk abubuwan da aka shigar suna tsayawa a cikin wannan mai binciken; babu abin da aka ɗora ko adanawa.

Ƙididdiga yankuna masu saurin zuciya

Ta yaya zan lissafta yankunan bugun zuciya? Saukewa:

Zaɓi hanya da haɓaka iyakoki shida. Kashi-na-mafi girma yana ninka iyakar bugun zuciyar ku ta kowane yanke. Karvonen yana ƙara wannan adadin ajiyar ajiyar zuciya (mafi girman ƙarancin hutun zuciya) zuwa ƙimar hutun ku. Ƙididdigar shekarun yana amfani da 220 rage shekarun; Ƙididdigar yawan jama'a ce kawai. An adana abubuwan shigar da bugun zuciya na? Saukewa:

A'a. Lissafin yana gudana a cikin wannan mai binciken. Ba a ɗora shekarunku, ƙimar zuciyar ku da yankewar yanki ta wannan kayan aikin ba. Saukewa:

Menene ma'anar Karvonen?

Yana ƙididdige ƙimar manufa azaman hutun bugun zuciya tare da zaɓin juzu'i na ajiyar yawan bugun zuciya. Ajiye shine matsakaicin adadin bugun zuciya banda hutun bugun zuciya. Wannan sakamakon har yanzu kimantawa ne, ba takardar sayan magani ba.

Shin rage shekaru 220 shine ainihin matsakaicin bugun zuciya? Saukewa:

A'a. Yana da ƙayyadaddun ƙididdigar yawan shekaru da Ƙungiyar Zuciya ta Amirka ta ambata, ba ma'auni na iyakar ku ba. Kwarewa, lafiya, magani da bambancin mutum na iya shafar bugun zuciya.

Shin yankuna masu saurin zuciya suna iyakan likita? Saukewa:

A'a. Waɗannan jeri na ƙididdigar ƙididdiga ne masu daidaitawa don ilimin gaba ɗaya. Likitan likitanci na iya taimakawa saita manufa na sirri, musamman tare da yanayin zuciya ko magani wanda ke shafar bugun zuciya. Saukewa:
