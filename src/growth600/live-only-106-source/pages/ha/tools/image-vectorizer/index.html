Hoton Vectorizer

Gano tambura masu sauƙi, gumaka, da zane-zane masu launi cikin hanyoyin SVG. Zaɓi iyakar launi da girman daki-daki, samfoti vector, sannan zazzage SVG.

An rage mafi tsayi gefen zuwa wannan pixels masu yawa ko ƙasa da haka. Babban daki-daki na iya ƙirƙirar babban SVG.

Mafi kyawun tambura, gumaka, da zane-zane masu launi. Wannan hanyar tana bin grid pixel; ba ya santsi mai lankwasa ko sake fitar da cikakkun bayanai na hoto.

Har zuwa 5 MiB kowane hoton tushe. Ana sarrafa hoton a cikin gida kuma ba a loda shi ko adana ta atomatik. Manyan hotuna na iya amfani da ƙwaƙwalwar na'urar; ana ba da shawarar kwamfuta.

Ta yaya kuke juya hoto zuwa SVG?

Zaɓi hoton raster, zaɓi dalla-dalla yadda ake fitarwa da ƙidaya launi, sannan ƙirƙirar SVG. Ƙungiyoyin kayan aiki masu launin pixel suna gudana zuwa ainihin hanyoyin vector; gefuna suna bin grid ɗin pixel da aka zaɓa, don haka zane mai lebur yana aiki mafi kyau fiye da hotuna.

Ta yaya kuke juya hoto zuwa SVG?

An loda hotona?

A'a. An yanke hoton kuma an gano shi a cikin wannan shafin mai lilo. Ba a loda shi ko adana ta atomatik.

Shin SVG ya ƙunshi ainihin hoton?

A'a. Sakamakon ya ƙunshi sifofin hanyar SVG, ba hoton raster da aka haɗa ba. Yana kusantar tushen ta hanyar haɗa pixels zuwa launuka da hanyoyi.

Shin zai sarrafa hoto lafiya?

A'a. An yi nufin alamar pixel-grid don sauƙaƙe tambura, gumaka, da zane-zane masu launi. Hotuna na iya samar da gefuna masu toshewa da manyan fayilolin SVG.

Shin kayan aikin yana lodawa ko adana hotona?

A'a. Ana sarrafa tushen a cikin wannan shafin kuma ba a loda shi ko adana ta atomatik. Gudun sarrafawa da amfani da ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya sun dogara da na'urarka.
