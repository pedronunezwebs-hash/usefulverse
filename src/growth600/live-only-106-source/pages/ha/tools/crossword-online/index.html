Shafin farko/Crossword Kan layi

Kyauta ne, babu asusu

Crossword Kan layi

Warware sabo, kalmar giciye da aka samar a cikin gida daga ƙaramin saiti na asali. Zaɓi alamar ko murabba'i, rubuta da madannai naka, kuma duba ko bayyana amsoshi lokacin da kake buƙatar taimako.

Amsoshi sunyi watsi da sarari da harka harafin. Saitin alamar yana da iyaka kuma na asali; sabon kwamiti yana sake tsara shigarwar sa amma bai yi alkawarin ciyarwar wasan caca mara iyaka ta yau da kullun ba. Saukewa:

: rubuta harafi, yi amfani da kibau don motsawa, kuma danna Backspace don share murabba'i. Saukewa:

Ketare

    Down

      Ta yaya zan kunna wannan giciye?

      Zaɓi alamar ƙasa ko ƙasa, sannan rubuta haruffa cikin murabba'ai da aka zaɓa. Yi amfani da maɓallin kibiya don matsawa cikin amsa. Bincika kalma ko duka wasanin gwada ilimi, bayyana harafi ɗaya ko cikakkiyar amsar, ko fara sabon allon shuffled.

      Ta yaya zan kunna wannan giciye?

      Zaɓi alamar ƙasa ko ƙasa, sannan rubuta haruffa cikin murabba'ai da aka zaɓa. Yi amfani da maɓallin kibiya don matsawa cikin amsa. Bincika kalma ko duka wasanin gwada ilimi, bayyana harafi ɗaya ko cikakkiyar amsar, ko fara sabon allon shuffled.

      An ɗorawa ko adana amsoshi na?

      A'a. Wasan wasa, amsoshi da ci gaba da ci a cikin wannan shafin mai bincike. Babu asusu, loda, sarrafa uwar garken ko adana tarihin wasa.

      Shin wannan kalmar giciye ta ƙunshi ciyarwar wuyar warwarewa mara iyaka ta yau da kullun? Saukewa:

      A'a. Shafin yana gina allon shuffled daga ƙarami, iyakataccen saiti na asali-da-amsa nau'i-nau'i. Ba ya debo ko da'awar ɗakin karatu na yau da kullun mara iyaka. Saukewa:

      Zan iya amfani da wannan giciye ba tare da loda komai ba? Saukewa:

      ya. Allo da amsoshinku suna gudana a cikin wannan shafin mai lilo. Kayan aikin baya loda wasan ku ko buƙatar asusu. Saukewa:

      Shin wannan mai yin kalami ne? Saukewa:

      A'a. Wannan shafin don kunna alamu ne. Yi amfani da keɓantaccen Maker keɓaɓɓen kalmomi lokacin da kuke son tsara naku nau'i-nau'i-na amsa-da-alamu. Saukewa:
