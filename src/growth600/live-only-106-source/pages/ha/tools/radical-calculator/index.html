Sauƙaƙa Tushen Murabba’i

Sauƙaƙe madaidaicin madaidaicin radicals ta hanyar cire cikakken iko, ko sake rubuta juzu'i don haka adadin sa bai ƙunshi tushen murabba'i ba. Sakamako daidai ne don abubuwan shigar lamba da aka goyan baya.

Shigar da duka lambobi. Radicands suna iyakance zuwa ± 1,000,000,000,000; tushen tushen shine 2-12. Ana goyan bayan radiyo mara kyau kawai don maƙasudai marasa kyau.

Wannan yanayin yana ɗaukar lamba / (madaidaicin × √radicand). Ƙididdigar ƙididdiga ba za ta iya zama sifili ba; radicand dole ne ya zama tabbataccen duka lamba.

Menene √72 ya sauƙaƙa zuwa?

√72 shine 6√2, saboda 72 = 36 × 2 da √36 = 6.
