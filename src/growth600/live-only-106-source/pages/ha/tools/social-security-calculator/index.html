Calculator Tsaron Jama'a

Yi ƙididdige fa'idar ma'aikaci mai ritaya ta yin amfani da matsakaicin adadin kuɗin da kuka samu na wata-wata (AIME), shekarar da kuka cika shekaru 62, da shekarun da'awar daga 62 zuwa 70. Wannan ƙima mai sauƙi ba ta samun damar rikodin Tsaron Tsaron ku.

Makin lanƙwasa 2025 da 2026 da 90% / 32% / 15% dabara sun fito ne daga SSA. Cikakken shekarun ritaya shine 67 ga waɗannan ƙungiyoyin. Wannan baya haɗa da gyare-gyaren farashin-rayuwa ko daidaitawa ga rikodin samun kuɗin ku.

Ta yaya wannan kimantawar Tsaron Jama'a ke aiki?

Hukumar Tsaron Jama'a tana aiwatar da ƙimar 90%, 32% da 15% zuwa sassan AIME. Makin lanƙwasawa ya dogara da shekarar da kuka fara cancanta. Misali, 2026 AIME na $5,825 yana ba da adadin inshora na farko (PIA) na $2,609.80 kafin daidaitawar shekaru.

Ta yaya wannan kimantawar Tsaron Jama'a ke aiki?

Hukumar Tsaron Jama'a tana aiwatar da ƙimar 90%, 32% da 15% zuwa sassan AIME. Makin lanƙwasawa ya dogara da shekarar da kuka fara cancanta. Misali, 2026 AIME na $5,825 yana ba da adadin inshora na farko (PIA) na $2,609.80 kafin daidaitawar shekaru.

Wannan kalkuleta yana samun dama ga asusun Tsaro na?

A'a. Baya neman lambar Social Security, shiga ko rikodin samun kuɗi. Abubuwan da kuka shigar ana ƙididdige su a cikin wannan burauzar kuma ba a ɗora su ko adana ta wannan kayan aikin ba.

Menene AIME a cikin Tsaron Jama'a? Saukewa:

AIME yana nufin matsakaicin adadin albashin wata-wata. SSA yana ƙididdige shi daga rikodin samun ma'aikaci bayan lissafin albashi da sauran dokoki. Wannan kayan aikin yana buƙatar AIME daga kimanta SSA ku; ba ya sake gina shi daga wasu kayan aikin albashi.

Menene Social Security lankwasawa?

Abubuwan lanƙwasa su ne madaidaicin dala da suka raba AIME zuwa kashi-kashi. Domin 2025 suna $1,226 da $7,391; na 2026 su ne $1,286 da $7,749. SSA yana shafi 90%, 32% da 15% ga ma'auni.

Da'awar a 62 ko 70 yana canza kimanta? Saukewa:

Ee. Don ƙungiyoyin 2025-2026 shekaru-62, wannan ƙididdigewa yana amfani da cikakken shekarun ritaya na 67, SSA's farkon da'awar rage da'awar, da kuma jinkirta kiredit na ritaya na kashi biyu bisa uku na 1% na wata-wata ta hanyar shekaru 70.
