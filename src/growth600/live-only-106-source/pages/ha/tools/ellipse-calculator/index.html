A'a. An ƙididdige lissafi a cikin wannan mazugi. Ba a aika ƙimar ku zuwa uwar garken ko adana ta wannan kayan aikin ba.

Ta yaya zan lissafta yankin ellipse?

Haɓaka π ta tsakiyar babban axis da ƙaramin ƙarami. Idan a da b sune semiaxes, yanki shine πab.
