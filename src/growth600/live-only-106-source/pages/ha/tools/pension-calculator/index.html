Shafin farko/Kalkuleta na fansho

Kyauta ne, babu asusu

Kalkuleta na fansho

Yi ƙididdige ƙayyadaddun fa'ida mai sauƙi na fansho daga albashin ƙarshe na fansho, shekarun sabis da ƙimar tarawa. Zabi misalin musanya wani ɓangare na fansho na shekara don jimlar jimlar.

Misali mai sauƙi, ba haƙƙin fensho ko shawarar kuɗi/haraji ba. Tsarin tsari, iyakoki, shekarun ritaya, indexation, fa'idodin tsira da haraji na iya canza sakamako. Yi amfani da abubuwan tarawa da haɓakawa daga takaddun tsari na hukuma. Kuɗi kawai ke tsara adadi; ba ya musulunta.

Ƙimar kwatanta

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige ƙimar fansho mai fa'ida?

Wannan kwatancin yana ninka albashin ƙarshe na fansho ta shekarun sabis da adadin kuɗin shekara-shekara. Misali, 50,000 × 30 × 2% an kiyasta 30,000 a kowace shekara kafin kowane gyare-gyare na musamman.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Ta yaya ake ƙididdige ƙimar fansho mai fa'ida?

Wannan kwatancin yana ninka albashin ƙarshe na fansho ta shekarun sabis da adadin kuɗin shekara-shekara. Misali, 50,000 × 30 × 2% an kiyasta 30,000 a kowace shekara kafin kowane gyare-gyare na musamman.

Ana loda adadin fensho na?

A'a. Duk ilimin lissafi yana gudana a cikin wannan mashigar. Ba a ɗora nauyin albashi, sabis da fensho ta wannan kayan aikin ba.

Nawa ne fensho shekaru 30 a kashi 2% akan albashin 50,000?

Amfani da dabarar fa'ida mai sauƙi, 50,000 × 30 × 0.02 yayi daidai da 30,000 a kowace shekara kafin ƙayyadaddun gyare-gyare na tsari.

Menene ma'anar canjin fensho?

Yana da ƙayyadaddun tsari da aka yi amfani da shi don ƙididdige jimlar kuɗi don musanya don barin wani ɓangare na fansho na shekara. Shigar da lamarin daga sharuddan makircinku na hukuma; ba kowa ba ne.

Shin wannan ya haɗa da harajin fensho ko ƙarin tsadar rayuwa?

A'a. Ba ya ƙididdige haraji, ƙididdigewa, hauhawar farashin kayayyaki, daidaitawa da wuri ko ƙarshen ritaya, fa'idodin tsira, matsakaicin albashi ko ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun tsari.

Shin wannan ya dace da kowane fansho?

A'a. Yana misalta ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun fa'ida guda ɗaya. Ƙayyadadden gudummawar tukwane, fenshon jihohi da tsare-tsare tare da dabaru daban-daban suna buƙatar nasu ƙididdiga na hukuma ko ƙididdiga masu ba da sabis.

Shafin farko

Kayan aikin burauza kyauta. Bayananka suna nan a na’urarka idan kayan aikin ya ba da dama.
