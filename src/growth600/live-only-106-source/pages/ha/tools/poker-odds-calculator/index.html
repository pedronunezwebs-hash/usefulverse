Poker Odds Kalkuleta

Shigar da katunan ramukan ku guda biyu, kowane katunan al'umma da adadin abokan hamayya. Cikakken allon kogi yana amfani da ƙididdiga daidai akan abokin gaba ɗaya; wasu yanayi suna amfani da siminti da za'a iya maimaita su a cikin wannan mai binciken.

Misali: Kamar yadda Kd. Daraja sai kwat; yi amfani da 10 ko T don goma.

Shigar da sanannun katunan 0-5, an raba su ta sarari ko waƙafi.

Ƙimar yiwuwar ilimi kawai, ba caca ko shawarar kuɗi ba. Ɗayan abokin gaba akan cikakken allon katin biyar an ƙididdige shi daidai (hannaye 990 mai yiwuwa). Wasu lokuta suna amfani da simintin gida mai iri. Sakamako baya hasashen hannu na gaske ko bada garantin nasara.

Yaya ake ƙididdige ƙimar Texas Hold'em?

Kalkuleta yana cire katunanku da allon bayyane daga daidaitaccen bene mai katin 52, sannan ya kwatanta mafi kyawun hannun karta mai katin biyar kowane ɗan wasa zai iya yi. Cikakken allo tare da abokin gaba ɗaya yana bincika kowane mai yiwuwa hannu mai kati biyu. Wasu lokuta suna amfani da samfurin Monte Carlo mai iri, don haka abubuwan shigar da iri iri ɗaya suna ba da ƙima ɗaya. Adalci ya haɗa da rabonku na tsagawar tukwane.

Ana loda katunan poker dina?

No. Tabbatar da kati da duk lissafin ƙididdiga suna gudana a cikin gida a cikin wannan mazugi. Ba a yi amfani da asusu, sabis na yin fare, binciken cibiyar sadarwa ko adana tarihin hannun da aka yi amfani da shi ba.

Menene ma'anar daidaiton poker?

Equity shine kiyasin rabon tukunya: nasara tana ƙidaya a matsayin cikakken rabo, kuma kunnen doki yana ƙidaya a matsayin juzu'i daidai ga kowane ɗan wasa da aka ɗaure. Ba daidai yake da kashi na nasara ba.

Yaushe ne rashin daidaiton poker daidai?

Tare da dukkan katunan al'umma guda biyar ganuwa da abokin gaba ɗaya, kayan aikin yana bincika kowane abokin adawar katin biyu hannun da ba a taɓa gani ba. Sauran yanayi suna amfani da ƙididdigar Monte Carlo iri kuma suna iya bambanta daga ainihin sakamakon.

Zan iya amfani da wannan don shawarar yin fare?

A'a. Yana kwatanta haɗin katin kawai a ƙarƙashin ƙa'idodin Texas Hold'em. Ba ya ƙirƙira yin fare, halayen abokan gaba, rake, dabarun gasa ko yanke shawara na gaba.
