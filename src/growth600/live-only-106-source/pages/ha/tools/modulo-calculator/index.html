Modulo Kalkuleta

Yi ƙididdige ƙimar Euclidean da saura ga duka lambobi. Ana sarrafa abubuwan shigar mara kyau akai-akai, kuma asalin rarrabuwa yana nuna yadda aka samar da amsar. Saukewa:

Lambar da za a raba; yana iya zama mara kyau.

Yi amfani da kowace lambar da ba ta sifili ba; cikakken darajarsa tana bayyana ma'anar ma'ana. Saukewa:

Bincika ko wannan ƙimar ta dace da rabon modulo mai rarrabawa. Saukewa:

Yana Karɓar dukkan lambobi waɗanda aka sanya hannu har zuwa lambobi 50. Babu juzu'i ko ƙima. Sakamakon yana amfani da Euclidean modulo; wasu harsunan shirye-shirye suna amfani da wata saura ka'ida don munanan abubuwa. Saukewa:

Sakamako

Ta yaya kuke lissafta modulo mai lamba mara kyau? Saukewa:

Wannan kalkuleta yana amfani da abin da ba mara kyau ba wanda ya ragu kaɗan fiye da cikakken mai rarrabawa. Misali, -7 mod 3 shine 2 saboda -7 = (-3 × 3) + 2.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Ta yaya kuke lissafta modulo mai lamba mara kyau? Saukewa:

Wannan kalkuleta yana amfani da abin da ba mara kyau ba wanda ya ragu kaɗan fiye da cikakken mai rarrabawa. Misali, -7 mod 3 shine 2 saboda -7 = (-3 × 3) + 2.

An loda lambobi na? Saukewa:

A'a. Madaidaicin lissafin yana gudana a cikin wannan mai binciken. Ba a loda ko adana lambobi. Saukewa:

Menene -7 mod 3? Saukewa:

Yin amfani da yarjejeniyar Euclidean, -7 mod 3 shine 2: -7 = (-3 × 3) + 2, da 0 ≤ 2 <3.

Menene ma'anar ma'anar ma'anar modolo m? Saukewa:

Lambobi biyu suna hade modulo m lokacin da aka raba bambancinsu ta |m|. Kwatankwacin zaɓi yana duba wannan daidai. Saukewa:

Me yasa wannan zai iya bambanta da % mai aiki da harshen shirye-shirye? Saukewa:

Wasu harsuna suna adana alamar ragi mara kyau a cikin ragowar. A maimakon haka wannan kalkuleta ya dawo da saura daga 0 har zuwa, amma bai haɗa da, cikakken mai rarrabawa ba. Saukewa:

Shafin farko

Kayan aikin burauza kyauta. Bayananka suna nan a na’urarka idan kayan aikin ya ba da dama.
