Cire Hotuna daga PDF

Zaɓi PDF don nemo hotunan raster da aka haɗa, duba su, da adana kwafin PNG daban ko ZIP ɗaya. Ba a sanya shafukan yanar gizo zuwa hotunan kariyar kwamfuta ba.

Abubuwan cirewa suna goyan bayan abubuwan hoto na raster; baya juya duka PDF shafuka zuwa hotuna. Zane-zane na vector da rubutun shafi ba hotunan raster bane daban. Saukewa:

Fayil ɗin ku yana kan wannan na'urar kuma ba a ɗora shi ba. pixels da aka ciro ana sake sanya su azaman PNG, don haka ainihin tsarin fayil ɗin da aka saka da metadata ba a kiyaye su. Saukewa:

Iyakoki na gida: 25 MiB PDF, shafuka 300, hotuna 200, megapixels 8 akan kowane hoto, megapixels 16 duka da 25 MiB ZIP. Manyan PDFs suna amfani da ƙwaƙwalwar na'urar; ana ba da shawarar kwamfuta. Saukewa:

Zazzage duk hotuna (ZIP)

    Ta yaya zan fitar da hotuna daga PDF?

    Zaɓi PDF kuma zaɓi Cire hotuna. Wannan burauzar tana karanta goyan bayan abubuwan da aka saka hoton raster kuma yana canza pixels ɗin su da aka yanke zuwa fayilolin PNG. Zazzage hotuna ɗaya ko ɗaya ZIP.

    Ta yaya zan fitar da hotuna daga PDF?

    Zaɓi PDF kuma zaɓi Cire hotuna. Wannan burauzar tana karanta goyan bayan abubuwan da aka saka hoton raster kuma yana canza pixels ɗin su da aka yanke zuwa fayilolin PNG. Zazzage hotuna ɗaya ko ɗaya ZIP.

    PDF nawa aka ɗorawa ko hotuna?

    A'a. Ana sarrafa PDF ɗin ku a cikin wannan shafin mai lilo. Kayan aikin baya loda fayil ɗin ko ajiye shi zuwa asusu.

    Wannan kayan aikin yana juya kowane shafin PDF zuwa hoto?

    A'a. Yana neman goyan bayan abubuwan hoto na raster. Yi amfani da kayan aikin PDF-zuwa hoto idan kuna son hoton da aka yi na kowane shafi duka.

    Shin PNG da aka fitar zai zama byte-for-byte daidai da fayil ɗin da aka saka?

    A'a. Mai binciken yana yanke bayanan abubuwan hoto da ke goyan bayan kuma ya sake yin rikodin pixels ɗin su azaman PNG a madaidaicin abin da aka yanke. Ba a adana ainihin matsi, tsarin fayil da metadata.

    Me yasa wasu hotuna na PDF bazai bayyana ba?

    Vector zane-zane, abin rufe fuska na hoto, sabon saƙon rubutu, kariyar kalmar sirri, fayilolin da ba su da kyau da iyakokin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiyar burauza na iya hana cirewa. Kayan aikin yana ba da rahoton abubuwan tallafi kawai kuma baya loda fayil ɗin ku.
