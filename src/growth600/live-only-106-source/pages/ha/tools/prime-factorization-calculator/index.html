Kalkuleta na rarraba lamba zuwa abubuwan farko

Rarraba ingantaccen lamba gabaki ɗaya zuwa manyan abubuwa. Dubi manyan iko, abubuwa masu maimaitawa, tsani rarrabuwa da yawan masu rarrabawa masu inganci. Lissafin yana gudana a gida a cikin burauzar ku.

Shigar da lamba daga 1 zuwa 1,000,000,000,000. Ƙirƙira ƙididdiga yana amfani da madaidaicin ƙididdiga a cikin wannan mazuruf.

Sakamako

Ta yaya kuke samun babban ƙima na lamba?

Raba ta mafi ƙanƙanta mafi ƙanƙanta wanda ke raba lambar, sannan a maimaita tare da adadin har sai ƙimar da ta rage ta zama firamare. Misali, 360 = 2³ × 3² × 5.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Ta yaya kuke samun babban ƙima na lamba?

Raba ta mafi ƙanƙanta mafi ƙanƙanta wanda ke raba lambar, sannan a maimaita tare da adadin har sai ƙimar da ta rage ta zama firamare. Misali, 360 = 2³ × 3² × 5.

An loda lambobi na?

A'a. Lissafin yana gudana a cikin wannan mai binciken kuma wannan kayan aikin baya lodawa ko adana bayanan ku.

Menene babban ma'auni na 360?

360 = 2³ × 3² × 5. Sigar maimaita-firamare shine 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5.

Masu rarraba nawa ne lamba ke da su?

Idan manyan ikon sa pᵃ × qᵇ, ƙididdigar masu rarraba shine (a + 1) (b + 1). Wannan kalkuleta yana nuna ƙidayar madaidaicin lamba.

Zai iya ƙima kowane girman lamba?

Wannan kayan aikin burauzar yana karɓar lambobi duka daga 1 zuwa 1,000,000,000,000. Manyan dabi'u suna waje da kewayon da aka bayyana.

Shafin farko

Kayan aikin burauza kyauta. Bayananka suna nan a na’urarka idan kayan aikin ya ba da dama.
