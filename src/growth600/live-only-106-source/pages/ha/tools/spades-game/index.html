Spades

Kunna cikakkiyar matches spades mai kujeru huɗu a cikin wannan mazugi. Kuna wasa Kudu tare da sauƙaƙan bots na gida guda uku. Bid dabaru ko nil, bi rigar kwat da wando, kuma amfani da spades a matsayin trump; spades ba zai iya kaiwa ba har sai ya karye sai dai idan kun riƙe spades kawai. Babu asusu ko abokan adawar hanyar sadarwa.

    Hannun ku

    Match result

    The three computer players use simple local heuristics, not expert strategy. This single-player version has no online multiplayer, partner chat, or persistent statistics.

    Nuna dokokin

    The standard 52-card deck is dealt evenly. Each player bids 0–13 tricks; a successful nil bid scores 100 and a failed nil loses 100. Team contracts add their non-nil bids: make the total for 10 points per bid, plus one bag for each extra trick; miss it and lose 10 points per bid. Every ten accumulated bags subtract 100 points. Players must follow the suit led when possible. Spades cannot be led until broken unless a player holds only spades, and a spade beats any non-spade card.

    Each overtrick is one bag. At ten bags, the team loses 100 points and keeps the remainder.

    Yaya kuke wasa Spades anan?

    Each player bids how many tricks they expect, including nil for zero. South plays against three simple bots. Follow the led suit when possible; otherwise you may play any card. Spades cannot be led until broken (unless your hand contains only spades), and they trump other suits. Team bids are scored together, overtricks are bags, and each ten bags costs 100 points.

    Shin wannan wasan Spades yana haɗuwa da wasu mutane?

    No. Three simple bots run in this browser. Cards, bids and scores are not uploaded or saved by UsefulVerse.
