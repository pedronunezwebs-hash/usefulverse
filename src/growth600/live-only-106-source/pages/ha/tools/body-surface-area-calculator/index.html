Shafin farko/Ƙirar Yankin Jiki

Kyauta ne, babu asusu

Ƙirar Yankin Jiki

Shigar da tsayi da nauyi don kwatanta kididdigar yanki guda biyu na gama-gari. Sakamakon tsari na iya bambanta; wannan bayanin lissafi ne, ba jagorar likita ba.

Ƙimar manya kawai. Kewayon tallafi: 50-250 cm da 10-500 kg, gami da daidaitattun sassan Amurka.

Kar a yi amfani da waɗannan ƙididdiga don allurai na magani, ganewar asali ko shawarwarin magani. Bi umarnin ƙwararrun ƙwararrun kiwon lafiya. Ba a yi nufin yara ba.

Mai yawan siyarwa: √(tsawo cm × nauyi kg ÷ 3,600). Du Bois: 0.007184 × nauyi kg^0.425 × tsawo cm^0.725. Sakamako kiyasi ne, ba ma'auni kai tsaye ba.

Kiyasin yanki na jiki

Ta yaya ake kiyasin filin jiki?

Mosteller yana amfani da tushen murabba'in tsayi a cikin nauyin sau centimeters a kilogiram da aka raba da 3,600. Du Bois yana amfani da ma'aunin tsayi-da nauyi daban. Dukansu sun dawo da kimantawa a cikin murabba'in mita.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Ta yaya ake kiyasin filin jiki?

Mosteller yana amfani da tushen murabba'in tsayi a cikin nauyin sau centimeters a kilogiram da aka raba da 3,600. Du Bois yana amfani da ma'aunin tsayi-da nauyi daban. Dukansu sun dawo da kimantawa a cikin murabba'in mita.

Ana loda ma'auni na?

A'a. Ƙididdiga tana gudana a cikin wannan mazuruftan. Ba a ajiye ma'auni ko lodawa ba.

Me yasa tsarin ke ba da sakamako daban-daban?

Kowace dabara tana yin tsayi da nauyi daban. Bambancin da aka nuna shi ne lissafi, ba ma'auni na wane tsari ya dace da wani mutum ba.

Zan iya amfani da filin jiki don lissafin adadin magani?

A'a. Kar a yi amfani da wannan shafin don zaɓar ko canza kashi. Yi amfani da umarni kawai daga ƙwararren ƙwararren kiwon lafiya.

Shin wannan kalkuleta yana aiki ga yara?

A'a. An gabatar da shi ne kawai a matsayin ƙididdiga na manya kuma baya aiwatar da tsarin ilimin yara ko fassarar asibiti.
