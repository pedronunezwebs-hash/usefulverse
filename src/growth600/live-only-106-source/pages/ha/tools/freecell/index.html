Kyauta ne, babu asusu

FreeCell

Matsar da duk katunan 52 zuwa tushen kwat da wando. Yi amfani da sel guda huɗu masu kyauta azaman sarari na ɗan lokaci, gina ginshiƙai masu canza launi, kuma tsara kowane babban motsi a kusa da buɗaɗɗen sel da ginshiƙai.

Bar komai don sabon yarjejeniyar bazuwar. Yi amfani da iri ɗaya don sake ƙirƙirar ma'amala iri ɗaya; yana tsayawa ne kawai a wannan shafin.

Moves: 0

Katunan tebur suna gina ƙasa a cikin mabanbantan launuka. Zaɓin gudu dole ne ya riga ya canza launuka kuma ya sauko da matsayi ɗaya. An iyakance supermove ta sel marasa kyauta a halin yanzu da ginshiƙan mataimaka.

Foundations suna haɓaka daga Ace zuwa Sarki a cikin kwat da wando. Babban katin ginshiƙi ko kati a cikin tantanin halitta kyauta ne kawai za a iya motsa shi.

Yaya kuke wasa FreeCell?

Gina kowane tushe daga Ace zuwa Sarki a cikin kwat da wando guda. A kan teburau, gina ƙasa cikin maɓallan launuka. Tantanin halitta kyauta na iya ɗaukar kati ɗaya. Kuna iya matsar da ingantacciyar hanyar saukowa mai canza launi azaman babban motsi kawai lokacin da sel mara komai da ginshiƙan mataimaka zasu iya tallafawa.

Shin FreeCell ya ceci wasana?

A'a. Wasan yana gudana a cikin wannan shafin mai lilo. Ba ya amfani da asusu, loda motsi ko ajiye wasa ta atomatik. Tsarin sake kunnawa zaɓin zaɓi ne kuma yana tsayawa kawai a cikin wannan shafin.

Katuna nawa ne FreeCell supermove zai iya motsa?

Babban iyaka shine (sel marasa kyauta + 1) wanda aka ninka ta 2 zuwa ikon ginshiƙan taimako mara komai. Ba a kirga ginshiƙin wurin a matsayin mataimaki lokacin da babu kowa. Gudun kuma dole ne ya kasance yana saukowa kuma ya zama ja da baki.

Zan iya motsa jerin kai tsaye a cikin FreeCell?

Ee, idan katunan sun riga sun samar da gudu mai canza launi mai saukowa kuma girmansa bai wuce iyawar sel marasa kyauta da ginshiƙan taimako ba. Wasan yana tilasta wannan iyaka.

Ana iya sake kunnawa na FreeCell?

Ee. Shigar da iri ɗaya na zaɓin zaɓi kuma zaɓi sabuwar yarjejeniya don sake fasalin fasalin ta a cikin wannan mazugi. Bar iri ba komai don shuffle bazuwar. Ba a aika iri ko wasa zuwa uwar garken ba.
