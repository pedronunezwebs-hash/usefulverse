Kyauta ne, babu asusu

Lissafin Furotin

Yadda gram na furotin a kowace rana daga nauyin jiki da nau'in gram-per-kilogram wanda kuka zaɓa. Raba sakamakon a cikin abinci ko kwatanta shi da abin da kuka shigar.

0.8 g/kg/rana shine babban babban abin da aka ba da shawarar Bayar da Abinci daga Makarantun Ƙasa. Bukatu sun bambanta da mutum. Wannan kalkuleta zai baka damar zaɓar wani abu kuma baya rubuta ɗaya.

Kimanin lissafi kawai, ba tsarin abinci ko shawarar likita ba. Bukatun furotin na iya bambanta da shekaru, lafiya, ciki, aiki, da sauran dalilai. Tambayi ƙwararren likita ko likitan abinci game da bukatun ku. Ƙimar kowane-abinci rabo daidai ne, ba maƙasudin abincin da aka ba da shawarar ba.

Ta yaya zan lissafta adadin furotin yau da kullun daga nauyin jiki?

Mayar da nauyin jiki zuwa kilogiram, sannan ku ninka shi da ma'aunin giram-kowa-kilogram da kuka zaba. Raba wannan kiyasin yau da kullun da adadin abinci don rarrabuwar lissafi.

An loda nauyi na ko ajiyewa?

No. Lissafin yana gudana a cikin wannan burauzar. Ba a ɗora nauyin ku da abin da ake ci ta wannan kayan aikin ba.

Me ake nufi da gram 0.8 a kowace kilogiram?

Yana nufin a ninka nauyin jiki a kilogiram da 0.8 domin samun giram kowace rana. Makarantun Makarantun Ƙasa sun lissafa 0.8 g/kg/rana a matsayin Ba da Shawarar Bayar da Abinci ga manya; Magana ce ta gaba ɗaya, ba manufa ɗaya ba.

Shin wannan kalkuleta yana gaya mini adadin furotin da nake bukata?

Yana yin lissafi tare da abin da kuka shigar. Shekaru, lafiya, ciki, aiki, da sauran yanayi na iya shafar buƙatu, don haka tambayi ƙwararren likita ko likitan abinci don jagorar kai.

Shin adadin kowane abinci shine shawarar sabis?

Yana kawai raba zaɓaɓɓen kimanta yau da kullun daidai da adadin abincin ku. Abincin gaske ba dole ba ne ya ƙunshi daidai adadin.
